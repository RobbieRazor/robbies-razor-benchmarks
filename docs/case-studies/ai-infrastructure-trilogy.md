# 1. Purpose

The purpose of the AI Infrastructure Trilogy is to compare how different layers of an AI system respond to increasing demand.

The three analytical layers are:

```text
Layer 1
Physical / facility infrastructure

Layer 2
Compute and hardware infrastructure

Layer 3
Software / inference architecture
```

The central question is not:

> Which company is brute-force?

The central question is:

> At each layer, what burden is being addressed, what resources are being added, what internal efficiencies are being achieved, what reusable structure is preserved, and what measurable benefit results?

# 5. Boundary Avoidance Requirement

Infrastructure growth alone is not Boundary Avoidance.

A bounded Boundary Avoidance analysis should demonstrate:

```text
identified internal burden
→ external expansion
→ burden remains unresolved or is displaced
→ measured internal improvement is insufficient
```

while considering:

- workload growth;
- capability gains;
- reliability;
- safety;
- redundancy;
- new services;
- alternative explanations;
- counterevidence.

Required distinction:

```text
more infrastructure
≠
Boundary Avoidance
```

```text

≠
```
